What Is the Golden Demo?

Origin

Requested directly by Marc Benioff after seeing three separate SlackFirst demos at the Hawaii offsite. He wanted a single unified vision projecting 12–18 months ahead to Dreamforce 2026.

The Vision

Slack as the Agentic OS for the enterprise — AgentForce, Slack, and third-party agents connected in a single cross-cloud story, all rooted in user research.

Safe harbor: This is an exploratory prototype. Nothing featured is on the product roadmap (yet) or representative of final UI.

The demo is not a commitment or replacement for existing Golden Demos from Solutions CoE. It's a visionary prototype.

Sales Cloud

SlackFirst Sales

Follow an Account Executive working a deal to close — using AI to automate updates, gathering quotes, getting approvals, and turning meetings into action.

Key Capabilities

  • Slack Voice Mode for seamless communication
  • Rich agent interactions & recommendations
  • Ambient Intelligence + meeting recaps
  • Approvals with AI context
  • Tableau metrics integration

Core Insight

"Speed is everything for sellers. The more we smartly automate — updating Salesforce, gathering quotes, getting approvals — the more they can focus on closing."

Sales vignette: an AE using cutting-edge AI throughout a deal cycle. Meetings are a key workflow — prep, during, and post-meeting follow-up.

Sales Cloud · Research Voices

What Sellers Actually Need

"One of my biggest gripes is updating Salesforce hygiene. In a perfect world: 'Push all opportunities with close dates in the next 7 days out by two weeks.' That would be the biggest use case."

"Can it create the opportunity Slack channel and pull the meeting together? Otherwise it's 20 min of finding time on people's calendars."

Broad Research Themes & Learnings

  • Lean into SlackFirst advantages. Deep context, everything in one place, immediate action — these are Slack's native strengths.
  • Think holistically about use cases. Meetings are high-leverage: solve before, during, and after. Seamless end-to-end solves the "work of work."
  • Ambient AI felt novel. Make behavior configurable — show when AI is listening, allow hiding when screen-sharing with customers.
  • Build trust over time. Users are skeptical from prior AI experiences. Start with humans in the loop; give tools for feedback and configuration.
  • Noise & channel sprawl reduce Slack's value. Be thoughtful about when channels/notifications are created — smartly archive when appropriate.
These themes emerged from Dreamforce research intercepts, two waves of interviews across Sales/Marketing/Service, and Engine customer feedback.
